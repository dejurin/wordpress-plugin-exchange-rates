## Recorded validation, with revision boundaries

- Primary technical-controls patch: its [review](technical-controls-phase1.md) records 235/235 Chromium scenarios on each of WP 5.6/PHP 5.6 and WP 7.1.3/PHP 8.3, plus WordPress, localization, packaging, upgrade and rollback evidence. These receipts apply to that narrower patch.
- Integrated lab: 92/92 Chromium scenarios, 9/9 focused scenarios and 20 HTML documents with zero validator errors were recorded.
- Native integrated recheck: Safari and Firefox each passed 92 full plus nine focused scenarios on that revision.
- Later duplicate-trend fix: Safari and Firefox each passed a new 16-case focused suite; actual VoiceOver excerpts confirmed single trend descriptions and return to the date button after Escape. The earlier full runs are not recast as full tests of this later revision.
- The October 28 accessibility audit identified additional contrast, color, label and localization work. Those findings are retained in [1.5.6](../roadmaps/1.5.6.md); they were not repaired or certified away in this checkpoint.

No new full browser or release acceptance suite was run merely to record this checkpoint. VoiceOver remains off; the user previously confirmed its AppleScript access was disabled after reopening the utility.
