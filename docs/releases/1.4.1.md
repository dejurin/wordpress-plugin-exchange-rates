## Limits and rollback

WordPress 7.2 is Beta 1 at this acceptance point. Compatibility is verified for this build; repeat on later Beta/RC and final releases. Keep `Tested up to: 7.1` until an eligible 7.2 RC, as required by the [Plugin Developer FAQ](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/plugin-developer-faq/#what-version-of-wordpress-should-the-tested-up-to-value-be). No minimum-version increase is introduced.

Browser results use Chromium. Safari, Firefox, physical devices, multisite, object caches and arbitrary third-party themes are not certified here. Theme simulations and exact legacy CSS comparisons do not establish a full theme-installation matrix. Synthetic providers do not establish production availability, freshness or CDN cache behavior. The earlier live canary establishes only its recorded three-hour local window.

For a site rollback, let in-flight work finish, deactivate 1.4.1, replace it with the verified 1.4.0 ZIP, reactivate and verify the hourly schedule plus saved data. Replacing active files alone does not restore the old recurrence. Do not delete settings/widgets/posts or rewrite the existing WordPress.org 1.4.0 tag. Public rollback is a separate corrective release operation; preserve the published history.
