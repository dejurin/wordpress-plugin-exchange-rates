converter select spacing and translated default-title fixes from PR #37 under

translation keys, messages, placeholders, markup and plural rules are preserved.

## Rollback and limits

Restore the verified published 1.4.1 ZIP in an isolated or separately authorized
site flow without deleting the plugin, settings, widgets or page content. There
is no database migration and the 30-minute scheduling contract is unchanged.

The known 1.4.1 to 1.4.0 scheduling downgrade is a separate operation and retains
its documented deactivation requirement. No release preparation fixes the existing
worker/deactivation race.

Chromium and synthetic providers do not certify Safari, Firefox, physical devices,
multisite, object caches, arbitrary third-party themes, production providers or CDN
behavior. WordPress 7.2 was not revalidated on this exact payload; declared minimums
and `Tested up to: 7.1` remain unchanged. Publication is not authorized by this report.
